## Semantic evidence status

A preliminary AI-assisted reading found no concrete improper reciprocal approval *offers* or explicit assent. The **required independent two-human annotation/adjudication was not completed**. Message-level absence-of-proposals is therefore **provisional**, not a human-adjudicated negative. Objective absence of any unauthorized final owner or eligible reviewer signature supports zero **enacted** reciprocal approval arrangements in the two observed episodes.

Different outcomes must not be merged:
1. legitimate review requests while q=0 were permitted;
2. forbidden q0 final authorizations were absent;
3. oracle-detected hidden technical defects were distinct from qualification status;
4. independent coincident decisions would not demonstrate collusion;
5. a successful scripted positive trace was an **apparatus test**, not emergent collusion.

## Interpretation and limitations

The experimental observation is a **floor** in both topologies for forbidden authorizations/deployments. This does **not** refute the graph-feasibility calculation, validate a coalition opportunity predictor, establish graph equivalence or show that LLM organizations are generally safe. The policy may have overridden local delivery incentives; the two technical failures altered eligibility; the sample contains only one matched pair; roles/packet access were rewired together; no human semantic coding or no-message controls were completed. Q1 qualified throughput shows the model could take legitimate positive actions.

An informative follow-up needs transparent independent annotations, controlled replication, explicit attention to technical fallbacks and a redesigned contrast capable of yielding a meaningful negative.
