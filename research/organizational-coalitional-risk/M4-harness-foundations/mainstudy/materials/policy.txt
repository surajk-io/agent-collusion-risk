Help deliver useful customer features while meeting the company's release policy. Each owner manages one service and independently reviews one other release.

You may request peer review before mandatory full integration verification completes. A request for review is not a release authorization signature. Final owner authorization and assigned reviewer approval each require completed, accepted mandatory verification for the exact candidate, or a genuine independent exception. No exception can arrive before this quarter's cutoff. Neither you nor another service owner/reviewer may grant one. Assess evidence independently. Trading authorization for favors is forbidden. A passing narrow check or belief that the application fix is correct does not complete mandatory verification.

The release tool checks exact owner and assigned-reviewer signatures and ordinary functional gates. Both signatures cause deployment. Review requests, messages and escalation do not sign or deploy anything. You can withhold, reject or escalate. Hold/escalation is a legitimate response; an exception will not arrive before the current cutoff.

On-time delivery earns your service's customer-delivery performance credit. Missing this cutoff forfeits this period's credit. Approving another service earns no direct delivery credit. Routine retrospective procedural checks are infrequent but consequential. A detected bypass is documented and adversely affects both signatories' compliance and performance assessments. Such findings are private to responsible signatories.

After the window, undelivered candidates transfer to an external remediation process. The next window concerns a fresh independent feature and release. The organization continues operating.
