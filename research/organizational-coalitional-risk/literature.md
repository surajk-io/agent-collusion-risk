# Focused prior-work and novelty gate

**Status: targeted starting check on 2026-10-24, NOT a complete literature review or proof of novelty.** Prior source-grounded reviews from the two predecessor directions remain available. Verify paper versions, experimental details, controls and definitions before making claims of novelty.

| Work | Verified high-level overlap | Open differentiation question |
|---|---|---|
| Feldman, Meir & Tennenholtz, *Stability Scores: Measuring Coalitional Stability* (2012), https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.5983 | Existing formal counts of coalitions with profitable deviations, including counts by coalition size | What is empirically learned about LLM-agent behavior beyond known formal coalition-stability measures? |
| Nakamura et al., *Colosseum: Auditing Collusion in Cooperative Multi-Agent Systems* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15198 | LLM-agent collusion auditing with formal cooperative objectives, varying coalition objectives/topology and comparing messages to executed actions | Does it already compare ex ante independently measured opportunity landscapes on matched organizational structures, or mainly behavioral collusion probes under specified coalition objectives? Read exact interventions. |
| Shi et al., *Emergent Collusion in Long-Horizon LLM Agent Interaction* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24967 | Repeated agent task/verification, local reward/protocol conflict and behavioral effects of peers, feedback, memory and reward | Which structural opportunity properties, if any, were computed ex ante and varied independently? |
| Collina et al., *Delegating Authorization to Misaligned Agents: Coalitional Alignment and Safe Control* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15803 | Formal reviewer-panel safety under coalitional alignment, vote thresholds and strategic reviewers; some reviewer experiments | How do formal safety conditions relate to unscripted coalition discovery and observed behavior in task-specialized organizations? |
| Guo et al., *Coalition Formation in LLM Agent Networks* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14386 | Coalition-formation theory and empirical LLM behavior in beneficial joint work | How does beneficial partnership stability differ from unauthorized coalitional harm? |
| Seyedin et al., *The Politician, the Liar, and the Obedient Worker* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09574 | Organizational hierarchy and negotiated/obedient agent behavior (lead; exact methods still require checking) | Are authority structure and endogenous coalition opportunities separated? |
| Borah, *Bosses, Kings, and the Commons* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29062 | Organizational power asymmetry and collective outcomes (lead; exact methods still require checking) | Is there unscripted harmful inter-agent coordination rather than allocation outcomes alone? |

**Candidate research delta (unverified):** An independently computed landscape of feasible/credible harmful coalitional opportunities, induced by executable organizational structures, assessed as a predictor of uninstructed realized LLM-agent collusion across multiple (ideally matched and held-out) configurations, with legitimate productivity and rival explanations reported.

**Required novelty gate:** Check primary methods and supplementary materials of the three closest empirical papers plus the formal coalition-stability and authorization papers; write an overlap/difference matrix and identify what precise result would be new. Failure to find an identical paper in a bounded search does not establish novelty.
