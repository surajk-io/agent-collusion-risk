Pending/unmet critical G2 items pause seeded and behavior-interpretable smoke progression until explicit researcher disposition; they do not prevent completing the already authorized fixed isolated pass unless there is a technical integrity/budget stop. Permissible review actions are to accept a semantically correct answer under the unchanged rubric, retain an unmet/uncertain criterion and its interpretation limit, or make an explicit decision about whether a mechanics-only smoke is appropriate. Any exception to a critical G2 progression rule must be explicitly approved and labeled, not silently treated as a pass. If the stimulus/adapter itself is defective, mark the affected observation compromised and correct the documentation/adapter only in a separately reviewed future revision; this battery provides **no adaptive retest, replacement run, threshold relaxation or new sampling authorization**.
