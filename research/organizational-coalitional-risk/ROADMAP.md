# Roadmap — organizational coalitional risk

**As of 2026-10-24:** One unified research program. **M0 focused differentiation/novelty check provisionally complete; M1 credible organizational workflow is current.** Prior bounded literature work and exploratory pilot are retained, but neither validates novelty or emergent collusion.

| Milestone | Completion criterion | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0. Differentiate from closest work | Compare primary methods of Colosseum, long-horizon collusion, hierarchy, institutional controls, corruption and authorization studies; record overlap, likely delta, strongest objection | **Provisional complete; revisit against chosen workflow** |
| **M1. Credible organizational workflow** | Small executable workflow with specialized tasks, local objectives plus organizational guideline, independent welfare, permissions, information, legitimate outcomes, harmful opportunities and non-collusive alternatives | **Current; none approved** |
| M2. Formal bridge | Derive feasible coalitional deviations independently from executable structure; define opportunity types, payoff/welfare distinctions, coalitional credibility, count rules and at least one matched intervention | Not started |
| M3. Behavioral protocol / causal controls | Competing predictions, matched configurations, baseline models, prompts, monitoring/evaluation cues, discovery/attempt/execution labels, positive and negative controls, legitimate productivity, sample/analysis plan and budget; human approval | Not started |
| M4. Implementation validation | Tool-enforced permissions, tests, logs, reproducibility, positive control and independent adversarial critique | Not started |
| M5. Pilot and investigation | Small pilot to validate construct and mapping; then adequately replicated tests with uncertainty and held-out organizational structures where feasible | Not started |
| M6. Synthesis and design implications | Report positive, negative and inconclusive findings, causal limits, external validity and productivity tradeoffs | Not started |

## Scope of approval

**Explicitly approved:** Unify the two previously separate directions; adopt their shared overarching research question; organize the repository around it. Distinct legitimate local agent tasks plus an organizational-welfare guideline is an intended setting, not an empirical finding.

**Not approved:** One metric, monotonic count hypothesis, topology as the only predictor, specific experiment, scenario, model, prompts, or reward scheme. Experimental decisions require the approval gates in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

## Near-term working sequence

1. **Use the completed focused novelty check** in [literature.md](literature.md), without overstating originality; revisit the most direct competing methods once a workflow is chosen.
2. Produce 1–2 small credible workflow sketches with roles, tools/permissions, objectives, observable welfare and realistic conflict; identify confounders. Present for human approval.
3. Choose one workflow, then derive a minimal opportunity landscape and verify the mapping before using LLMs.
4. Test whether a controlled structural contrast with meaningful nontrivial observable agent behavior is possible before investing in large-scale simulation.

## Stop / pivot conditions

Reassess if the apparent opportunity count is arbitrary/duplicates supersets, the formal environment can only make harm mechanically impossible, collusion cannot be separated from coordinated mistakes, the candidate effect is merely a role/prompt cue, or closest prior work already establishes the claimed result.
