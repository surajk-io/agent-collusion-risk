## Immediate M1 review gate

Read **[M1 candidate scenario and mandatory review gates](M1-design-review.md)**. Two reciprocal reviewer pairs versus one four-agent directed review cycle with fixed per-task two-signature requirements is the **working proposal to scrutinize**, not a finalized treatment. Explicit review of detailed objectives, starting conditions, policy/tool distinction, agent-facing messages, communication, timing and outcome validity **must precede implementation or data collection**.

The bounded novelty audit is complete as research work; novelty of a specific experiment/result is not established, and closer code-review/reciprocity literature must inform its claims.

## Scope of approval

**Explicitly approved:** Unify the two previously separate directions; adopt their shared overarching research question; organize the repository around it. Distinct legitimate local agent tasks plus an organizational-welfare guideline is an intended setting, not an empirical finding.

**Not approved:** The proposed four-agent scenario as a final experiment, any metric, monotonic count hypothesis, topology as the only predictor, model, prompts, reward scheme, tools, timeline, sample plan or behavioral claims. Experimental decisions require the approval gates in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

## Stop / pivot conditions

Reassess if the apparent opportunity count is arbitrary/duplicates supersets, the formal environment can only make harm mechanically impossible, collusion cannot be separated from coordinated mistakes, the candidate effect is merely a role/prompt cue, or closest prior work already establishes the claimed result.
