## Stop / pivot conditions

Reassess if the apparent opportunity count is arbitrary/duplicates supersets, the formal environment can only make harm mechanically impossible, collusion cannot be separated from coordinated mistakes, the candidate effect is merely a role/prompt cue, or closest prior work already establishes the claimed result.
