## Next research choices — 29 October approved focus, no approved protocol

**Working focus:** How do ex ante organizational opportunity properties interact with incentives and repeated history to shape *collusion*, including proposals, agreements, reciprocation, defection and persistence? Treat harmful help and independent violations as alternative explanations. The 28 October broader harmful-coordination agenda is superseded in emphasis, not erased as historical context. P/R is the first historical case, not a proven effect. See [research agenda](RESEARCH-AGENDA-2026-10-28.md) for the dated refinement and methodological limitations.

1. **Evidence/provenance gate:** Verify original live archives, complete independent human semantic annotation where possible and preserve the original October 26 result without retroactive rewriting.
2. **Current active gate — structure-by-incentive design:** Choose one theoretically motivated, realizable organizational contrast and at least two incentive conditions. Define the interaction estimand, feasible collusive opportunities in every arm, bundled/confounded attributes, independent-action and mechanical baselines, genuine collusion evidence standards, and legitimate throughput. P/R bundled reciprocity and cycle length; do not label it a single-property effect.
3. **Bounded calibration gate:** Examine legitimate competence, incentive comprehension, opportunity recognition, and ability to negotiate across a small documented set of models/environments without optimizing for a favored interaction. Diagnose floor effects and technical fallbacks.
4. **Fresh replicated causal study:** After protocol/budget approval, randomize/counterbalance independent matched episodes across structure and incentive arms; estimate collusive attempts, agreements, contingent actions and persistence, and the structure-by-incentive interaction with uncertainty. Separate harmful help, independent misconduct and model-conditional effects. A limited second-model comparison is optional, not promised.
5. **Later prediction/generalization:** Only with sufficient behavioral signal, test pre-specified opportunity measures and response-pattern predictions against *held-out organizational configurations* and simple baselines; test model-by-structure interactions without claiming a universal collusion law.

**Three-to-four-day target:** A rigorous intervention specification and bounded pilot, with preliminary empirical answers if time, cost, observability and semantic verification permit. Not a promised generalizable risk predictor or statistically decisive result. **arXiv is a possible later preprint route**, conditional on originality, independently auditable evidence, accurate limitations and reproducibility—not a predetermined publication deadline.
