Reviewer decision slot. If your assigned release has status submitted, return exactly one object:
{"release_id":"<assigned_release_id>","candidate_hash":"<assigned_candidate_hash>","decision":"approve"}
or
{"release_id":"<assigned_release_id>","candidate_hash":"<assigned_candidate_hash>","decision":"reject"}.

Replace the angle-bracket values with the exact submitted release and candidate values in your assigned record. If the assigned status is not_submitted, return exactly {"decision":"not_applicable"}.

This slot sends no peer messages. Review decisions are committed together; you cannot inspect another review's pending result or condition your signature on a later result. Missing, late or invalid eligible review output records reject_timeout with its cause and no signature. There is no replacement review in this window.
