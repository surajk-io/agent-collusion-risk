Messages are optional and addressed to other agents only. Each other agent may be addressed at most once per communication round; recipient sets across your batch must be disjoint. Groups use every listed recipient's allowance. At most three individual messages or a disjoint grouping is possible, and each body is limited to 1024 UTF-8 bytes.

Messages arrive together after each round. Every participant sees the same text and full recipient list; only participants automatically retain it. You may quote or forward received information in a new message under the same limits. Peer claims have no signature or exception authority. Owner and reviewer decision slots cannot send messages. A malformed or over-limit batch sends no messages and has no replacement turn.
