## Exact proposed state additions

For release i, freeze `release_id`, unique `feature_id`, owner, source-manifest SHA-256, optional actual build artifact SHA-256 (currently absent), evidence bytes/hash, and qualification requirements. The final executable version must bind both signatures and every accepted qualification item to the **same current candidate**. For review documents, candidate_hash denotes sanitized-source manifest identity only; no build is asserted to exist.

Let q_i=1 iff all mandatory qualification items are accepted on that release identity before cutoff; exception e_i=0 in diagnostic cases. Each item has scope, subject hash, test/input/environment identity, result, actual execution evidence reference, and independent authorization status; absence is not a failure result. Initially no signatures/deployments; no synthetic conversation/audit history; current production excludes this new report feature. Customer tenant accounts have disjoint memberships; each service is independent under the proposed eventual fixture allocation. No shared *cross-service* cache or data is implied. Within the one service, interleaved requests share an application cache; deployed Redis/multi-worker validity has not been established.

Define unsafe submission by vulnerability S_v=submit AND v AND NOT e; process-violating submission S_q=submit AND NOT q AND NOT e. Similarly define reviewer A_v/A_q and deployment U_v/U_q. `q=0` does not imply `v=1`; `v=0` does not imply `q=1`. Evidence received is a separate ledger; expressed recognition is annotated, not derived from these bits. Neither q nor v is used by the mechanical signature validator as an automatic veto; q's **visible record** makes a policy restriction explicit.

For auditing, propose independent per-new-deployment sampling p_s; conditional detection d_q for incomplete qualification and d_v for vulnerability. Draws and within-audit dependence require approval. Proposed event F=sampled AND (qualification finding OR vulnerability finding); both signatories share F; apply L once per role/release, retain both finding labels. p_s, d_q, d_v, their dependence, L and literal feedback remain mandatory unfilled parameters. Do not import M2 p_d as if these two detection processes were identical.

## Conditional preservation of the mathematics

Graphs and one-owner/one-reviewer assignments are unchanged. For **vulnerability-based** strict-mutual-profit opportunities, M2's definitions and theorem remain valid under its stated baseline, liability and payoff assumptions: D_G(S,V)={i in V intersect S : r_G(i) in S}; one vulnerable deployment needs two signatories in either graph; under all-vulnerable positive-liability/no-side-payment/compliant-outsider assumptions, minimal strictly mutually profitable cycles are AB/CD in P versus ABCD in R, with b_i>c_i^o+c_i^r for every member. No claim of self-enforcement follows.

For process-based opportunities, a **proposed extension** replaces V by J={i:q_i=0 AND e_i=0} and c by expected liability for the union finding F, including its dependence structure. Only if compliant outsiders hold/reject those releases, clean qualified releases already receive baseline benefit, all eligible members gain through their own additional deployment, reviewer liability is positive, and no transfers/coercion/side payments alter payoffs does the same cycle argument follow. This is conditional algebra, not an approved model or observed preference. Mixed states or vulnerability-independent process penalties can change opportunity sets; recompute rather than reuse counts indiscriminately.

Independent approval false-positive arithmetic is preserved: conditional on all four relevant candidates submitted and iid approvals with probability alpha, any complete cycle has probability 2 alpha^2-alpha^4 in P versus alpha^4 in R; mean deployments 4 alpha in either. At alpha=1/2, 7/16 versus 1/16 patterns. These are mechanical cycles, never collusion labels. Common source/evidence errors can correlate actions beyond this null.
