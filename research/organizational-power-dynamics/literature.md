# Organizational power dynamics — literature starting points

**Status (2026-10-24): Dedicated literature review NOT completed.** These are leads from a broader collusion search and require examination of primary papers before specific claims or novelty judgments.

- Nakamura et al., *Colosseum: Auditing Collusion in Cooperative Multi-Agent Systems* (2026): coalition roles and communication topology. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15198
- Seyedin et al., *The Politician, the Liar, and the Obedient Worker* (2026): hierarchy, management incentives, private deals. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09574
- Borah, *Bosses, Kings, and the Commons* (2026): asymmetric organizational power and resource outcomes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29062
- Collina et al., *Delegating Authorization to Misaligned Agents: Coalitional Alignment and Safe Control* (2026): authorization and safety under reviewer coalitions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15803
- Guo et al., *Coalition Formation in LLM Agent Networks* (2026): coalition formation for beneficial collaboration. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14386

**Review priorities:** Formal definitions of authority, veto power, coalition power, influence and information control; empirical studies manipulating these properties; spontaneous vs instructed collusion; causal evidence and confounders; concrete research gaps.

Do not treat absence of findings from this initial list as proof of novelty.
