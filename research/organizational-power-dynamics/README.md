# Organizational power dynamics

**Question:** How does distribution of authority, permissions, information, responsibility and influence among AI agents affect the formation, execution, detectability and success of harmful coalitions?

**Status:** Open direction. A **dedicated literature review has not yet been conducted**, no formal power metric or experiment is approved. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

Original proposal: represent an agent organization as a directed graph of roles, decision authority, dependencies, information and influence (a DAG only when justified). Investigate how **pooled permissions** or asymmetric power change what subsets can do that individuals cannot.

Power is not a single scalar: an enforceable veto, approval authority, control of resources, access to information and persuasion are different mechanisms. Graph centrality does not automatically imply executable control.

Open hypotheses/questions (not adopted conclusions): Do centralized or decentralized approval rights change collusion? Do complementary permissions permit harmful behavior no agent can independently cause? Do powerful agents recruit coalitions or cause mere obedience? Can power-based measures predict harmful coordination beyond profit-only predictors?

**Identification risks:** Authority changes also alter information, incentives and action feasibility. Harmful joint behavior may reflect ordinary cooperation, compliance or independent errors, not collusion.

**Next:** Dedicated critical literature investigation on organizational hierarchy, authorization, power, information, multi-agent coordination and collusion. Brainstorm workflows in parallel if useful; do not claim novelty before checking closest work.
