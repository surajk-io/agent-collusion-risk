# First organization: author's model worksheet

**Status: intentionally unfilled.** The researcher will design the first organization rather than delegating scientific choices to an assistant.

## Task

Construct a concrete 3–4-agent organization in which members have distinct legitimate responsibilities, at least one subgroup can jointly benefit from a deviation that harms organizational welfare, and no member of that subgroup can produce the same harmful outcome alone.

Record:

1. Agents and their legitimate responsibilities.
2. Permitted actions and execution order.
3. What each agent can observe; communication channels.
4. Organizational objective W and a measurable legitimate baseline.
5. Individual incentive functions u_i, including their relation to W.
6. A concrete feasible joint deviation and its outcome.
7. Whether the deviation benefits *every* member, only some members, or requires transfers.
8. Whether an individual can betray the coalition profitably.
9. Whether independent behavior might produce the same outcome.
10. How a reviewer could distinguish attempted, realized, and causally coordinated harm.

## Initial tests to think through

- Enumerate feasible action profiles and utilities.
- Check that the designated harmful deviation is really impossible for a single agent.
- Hold outside agents' behavior fixed explicitly if using a static approximation.
- Find a counterexample or ambiguity in the first definition of collusion.
- Decide whether the environment can be executed with constrained tools rather than relying on model text to enforce permissions.
