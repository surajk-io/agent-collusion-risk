# Roadmap — organizational power dynamics

**As of 2026-10-24: Current milestone M0 — dedicated literature review.** Scenario exploration can proceed alongside M0, but no design has been approved.

| Stage | Completion criteria | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **M0. Critical literature and framing** | Verify closest work on hierarchy, approval/veto powers, authorization, influence, asymmetric information and AI-agent collusion; refine open question | **Current — dedicated review missing** |
| M1. Credible organizational workflow | Small real-world-plausible workflow with distinct enforceable powers, legitimate purpose and competing incentives; identify non-collusive alternatives | Exploring, none selected |
| M2. Formal power model | Precisely map roles, permissions and edges to executable actions and outcomes; specify incentives and welfare; verify edge cases | Not started |
| M3. Falsifiable intervention | Define an authority/permission contrast, controls for incentives/information, observable collusion criteria, rival explanations; obtain human approval | Not started |
| M4. Implementation validation | Tool-enforced permissions, tests, reproducible prompts and independent adversarial critique | Not started |
| M5. Empirical tests | Reproducible multi-agent comparisons, causal checks, uncertainty, alternative explanations | Not started |
| M6. Synthesis | What is supported, contradicted or unresolved, with limits and implications | Not started |

**Next deliverable:** A source-grounded critical literature review, recording evidence, gaps and key objections in [literature.md](literature.md). This is not yet a completed stage.
