# Focused novelty audit — organizational coalitional risk

**Checkpoint: 2026-10-24. Status: M0 COMPLETE (bounded method-level audit).** This is completion of the agreed literature-check milestone, **not** a proof of novelty or of the future experiment. This audit searched the existing Undermind workspace (including a previously completed 95-paper relevance search), used two new targeted semantic searches, read the methods and supplementary/appendix material of eight high-priority full-text papers with Undermind, and cross-checked public primary arXiv records. Consensus was requested but **unavailable**: monthly 30-search allowance exhausted; resets 2026-11-01. **No Consensus synthesis was completed.**

## Locked question, not a proved hypothesis

> How do organizational structures shape the opportunities for harmful coordination among task-specialized AI agents, and to what extent do properties of those opportunities predict realized collusion and organizational harm?

Agents perform legitimate local tasks and are instructed to benefit organizational welfare, but their local operational metrics may conflict with independent organizational welfare. They are **not** assumed to share identical utility functions, or to be spontaneously self-interested.

Central test candidate: derive an **ex ante coalition opportunity landscape** from executable permissions, task incentives, information and a legitimate welfare baseline (counts of distinct feasible harmful deviations, coalition overlap and pivotality, coalition sizes, gain/loss distributions, commitment, information/discovery/coordination requirements). Assess whether this landscape predicts *uninstructed* harmful coordination in held-out organizational configurations above cheap baselines (e.g., raw permission counts, coalition size, organization task success, model, prompt salience). Do not presume scalar sufficiency or monotonic effects.

## Narrowest defensible candidate delta

**Provisional:** No exact combined study was identified in this bounded audit that (1) derives **distinct, feasible, and strategically relevant harmful multi-agent deviations ex ante from enforceable organizational structure and specialized task incentives**, (2) varies or matches multiple such structures on opportunity properties, and (3) **prospectively evaluates those properties against causally supported, uninstructed, realized LLM-agent collusion** on unseen structures while also measuring legitimate productivity.

This is NOT a novelty proof. A new experiment about 'power affects collusion,' coalition topology alone, raw counts alone, prompt-only institutional warnings, or mechanical access-control constraints would substantially reproduce known ingredients.

## Critical objections and falsifiable discriminations

1. **Structural tautology:** Fewer feasible harmful actions mechanically imply fewer successful harmful actions. Separate *attempted coordination* and *successful action*; require feasible alternatives in both matched arms where testing behavioral propensity.
2. **Counting artifact:** Number of agent subsets is not number of distinct deviations. Supersets and duplicated exploits can explode the count; specify a defensible minimal coalition/action equivalence scheme before use.
3. **Preferences and credibility:** Task objectives or numeric rewards aren't intrinsic LLM utilities. Explicitly test whether opportunities are understood, attractive, and jointly credible without cueing that agents should collude.
4. **Confounding/mediation:** Changing permissions also changes salience, agent information, communication, authority perception, feasibility, and task efficiency. Opportunity descriptors may be correlated and not a sufficient mediator. Matched contrasts and independent prompt/knowledge controls are central.
5. **Collusion label:** Shared harm, acquiescence, false acceptance, and honest coordinated mistakes are not themselves evidence of a covert group-level unauthorized objective. Use communication/channel and counterfactual peer controls when feasible.
6. **Prediction generalization:** A few hand-picked structures can induce spurious trends. Score predictions out-of-structure on holdouts and compare simple baselines with uncertainty; negative or null results with functional positive controls are informative.
7. **Legitimate welfare and efficiency:** Monitor organizational performance and workflow delay; 'prevent everything' is not a safety success.

**M0 completed as a bounded literature investigation, not an established novel result.** Avoid another undirected survey. **M1 is now the binding risk:** identify a believable workflow and ensure the induced opportunity landscape is independently computable and can be manipulated without prescripting collusion. Not a proof that minimal reciprocal cycle length alone causes any effect, or that broad ex ante landscape metrics generalize.
